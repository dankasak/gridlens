How it works

Up and running in minutes

1

Install the Grid Lens App

Add the Grid Lens repository to your Home Assistant App store and install the integration. It hooks into your existing Energy dashboard sensors automatically.

2

Connect your current plan

Enter the details of your current electricity plan during setup. This is your free baseline — Grid Lens will model it immediately.

3

Compare all plans (optional)

Subscribe for $1/month to unlock all plans available in your state, updated continuously by our team. The optimizer runs locally — the API only delivers plan data.

4

View your dashboard

A custom Home Assistant dashboard is added to your sidebar automatically — live energy flow, forecast vs. actual power, optimal usage, cost estimates, and bill projections for each plan over any date range you choose. No cards to configure, no YAML.

5

Hand over the controls (optional)

Everything automated is opt-in and off by default. Turn on battery control, or control for an individual appliance, whenever you're ready — and take it back with one switch.
